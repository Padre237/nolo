Technical Application

This chapter describes how the GPS may be accessed, deployed, and used in practice,
while remaining within its intended scope and respecting SNOMED CT licensing
requirements.
The guidance in this chapter focuses on safe, predictable, and license-compliant use of
SNOMED CT identifiers and terms.

Accessing the Global Patient Set
The GPS is made available by SNOMED International for download under a Creative
Commons -NoDerivatives 4.0 International Public License

(CC BY-ND 4.0).

Access to the GPS:

• does not require SNOMED CT Membership
• does not require a SNOMED CT Affiliate License
The GPS is accessed via the GPS page on the SNOMED International website.
This distribution mechanism allows SNOMED International to:

• publish authoritative GPS releases
• notify users of updates and changes
The GPS is distributed as a standalone release artifact and may be used independently of
the full SNOMED CT release.
