## 6. Synthetic data

Facilities, stocks, accounts, licences, surveillance series and trend counters (`donnees/*.json`, `kb/data/`, and the demonstration data in `app/js/domain/`) are **fictitious**. They were generated for this project and released under MIT. They do not describe real people or real establishments.
